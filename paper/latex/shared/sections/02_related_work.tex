\section{Related Work}

The related-work review will cover pedestrian-dynamics research workflows, evidence-grounded language-model systems, retrieval-augmented generation \cite{lewis2020retrieval}, and reproducible evaluation practices. Literature discovery, screening, quality assessment, full-text acquisition, and corpus inclusion must remain separately documented stages. If a systematic-review claim is made, its reporting should follow an explicit protocol such as PRISMA 2020 \cite{page2021prisma}.
