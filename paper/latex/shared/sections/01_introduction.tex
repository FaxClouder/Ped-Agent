\section{Introduction}

Pedestrian-flow research combines empirical observation, physical measurement, simulation, and evidence synthesis. Classical models such as the social-force formulation provide a foundation for describing collective motion \cite{helbing1995social}, while modern research workflows must also manage a growing body of heterogeneous literature and experimental artifacts.

Ped-Agent is designed as a research assistant rather than an autonomous source of scientific truth. Its intended role is to retrieve governed evidence, expose provenance, support reproducible analysis, and close with insufficient evidence when claims cannot be justified. This manuscript will evaluate whether those design choices improve research traceability and task performance without conflating implemented software capability with validated scientific effectiveness.

The final introduction should state the concrete research gap, research questions, contributions, and scope after the evaluation protocol is frozen. Claims in this draft remain provisional.
